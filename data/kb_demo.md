# Knowledge base — DEMO (HomeFoodPro restaurant)

> This is the demo knowledge base. To use it: stop the bot, copy this file over
> `data/kb.md`, restart, then run the DEMO_SCRIPT.md recording.
> Every fact below is what the bot is allowed to say. Nothing else.

## About us
HomeFoodPro — home-cooked Iranian meals delivered in Mashhad. Family recipes, made to order each day, never frozen.

## Hours
We take orders every day, 10:00 to 21:00 (Iran time, UTC+3:30).
Delivery windows are 18:00 to 22:00.
Order at least 3 hours before your delivery window.

## Menu & prices
- Ghormeh Sabzi with rice — 185,000 Toman
- Gheymeh with rice — 175,000 Toman
- Chicken Sultani (1 skewer + rice + grilled tomato) — 240,000 Toman
- Mixed appetizer platter — 95,000 Toman
Prices include delivery inside Mashhad city.

## Delivery areas
All of Mashhad inside the city ring road.
Outside the ring road: ask us first — we will tell you if we can deliver and the extra cost.

## Ordering
Message us with how many portions and your address. We confirm by message and
send a tracking note when the courier leaves.

## Payment
Cash on delivery, or card-to-card to the number we send with your order
confirmation. We send the exact details when your order is confirmed.

## Allergens
Our rice is gluten-free. The marinades on the Chicken Sultani contain yogurt.
If you have an allergy, tell us and we will check with the kitchen before you
order — we will not guess about ingredients.

## Reservations
We are delivery only and do not take table reservations. For pickup, message us
and we will have it ready at the time you choose.

## Tone
Warm and respectful, like a family member. Short messages. Answer in the
language the customer wrote in (Persian or English).

## Catering & large orders
We do **not** cater events or large gatherings. We are a home kitchen with
daily capacity for family orders only. If a customer asks about catering, an
event, or an order above 20 portions, do not quote a price — tell them it is
outside what we do and hand the conversation to the owner.
