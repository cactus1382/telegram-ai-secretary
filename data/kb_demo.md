## About us
HomeFoodPro — home-cooked meals delivered in Mashhad. Family recipes, made to order, no frozen ingredients.

## Hours
We take orders every day, 10:00 to 21:00 (Iran time, UTC+3:30). Delivery is 18:00 to 22:00.

## Menu & prices
- Ghormeh Sabzi with rice — 185,000 Toman
- Gheymeh with rice — 175,000 Toman
- Chicken Sultani (1 skewer + rice + grilled tomato) — 240,000 Toman
- Mixed appetizer platter — 95,000 Toman
Prices include delivery inside Mashhad city.

## Ordering
Order by message at least 3 hours before your delivery window. Tell us how many portions and your address. We confirm by message and send a tracking note when the courier leaves.

## Payment
Cash on delivery, or card-to-card to the number we send with your order confirmation. We will send the exact details when your order is confirmed.

## Delivery areas
All of Mashhad inside the city ring. Outside the ring, ask us first and we will tell you if we can deliver and the extra cost.

## Tone
Warm and respectful, like a family member. Short messages. Answer in the language the customer wrote in (Persian or English).

## Policies
- We can change or cancel an order up to 2 hours before the delivery window at no cost.
- If something is wrong with an order, tell us the same day and we will replace it or refund it.
- We do not take orders for more than 20 portions by message; for bigger orders we will call you.
