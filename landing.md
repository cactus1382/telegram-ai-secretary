## The problem

You posted something. You went to sleep. By morning there are 14 DMs.

- *"How much for the course?"* — answered by nobody.
- *"Are you available this week?"* — answered by nobody.
- *"Does it work on iPhone?"* — answered by nobody.

Every one of those was a customer who was ready to pay, and who messaged two other people while waiting for you. They bought from whoever replied first. Research on this is blunt: a large majority of unanswered DMs never get a second message from the customer. The sale doesn't wait — it leaves.

You don't need a chatbot that sounds like a chatbot. You need someone who answers *as you*, at 2am, and never quotes a price you didn't authorize.

## What it does

An AI bot that connects to your Telegram **Business account** and answers DMs on your behalf, in your voice.

- **It replies as you.** Customers see your name and your avatar. Secretary Mode answers from your account, not from a bot profile.
- **It cannot lie.** It answers only from a knowledge document you write — your FAQ, your prices, your hours, your refund policy. Ask it something that isn't in there and it refuses to guess. It tells you to handle it yourself.
- **You stay in control.** Default mode drafts every reply and sends it to you first. Approve, edit, regenerate, or discard with one tap. Nothing goes out without you. When you trust it, flip to auto mode and it answers instantly and logs everything to you.
- **It answers in any language the customer writes in.** Russian, Arabic, Persian, English — it matches them.
- **It works with any AI provider.** OpenAI, OpenRouter, or your own gateway. You are not locked into one vendor.

## How it makes you money

Fast replies win sales. The customer who DMs five sellers at midnight buys from the one who answers before the others wake up. This is the one that answers.

It also costs almost nothing to run. A small model on a cheap API handles hundreds of DMs for a dollar or two.
